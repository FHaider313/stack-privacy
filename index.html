STACK Workout & body-weight tracker

Privacy Policy

A short policy for a short list of data — because STACK keeps your training on your phone and nowhere else.

Effective 30 October 2026 App: STACK (version 0.1.1) Package: com.furqanhaider.gymapp
+

Your training data never leaves your device. There is no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. The only information STACK sends anywhere is what is needed to check your one-time purchase, handled by Google Play and RevenueCat.

01

What STACK stores

STACK is built to work offline, using a local database (SQLite) that lives only on your phone. The data you enter falls into these categories:

  • Workout data — the exercises, weights, reps and cardio you log, plus the day you logged them, and any calories burned you choose to type in.
  • Body-weight data — the weigh-ins you choose to record, and your preferred unit (kg or lb).
  • Your name (optional) — if you enter a name, it is only used to greet you inside the app.

Nothing else is asked of you. There's no email, date of birth or sign-up, because STACK has no account system.

02

Where your training data goes

Nowhere. STACK has no backend server, no cloud sync and no login, so there is nothing for your training data to be sent to. It is written to your device's local storage and read back from that same storage. Uninstalling the app removes it. If your phone has its own backup switched on, your phone's backup may include the app's data, under Google's terms.

03

Purchases and third parties

After a 7-day free trial, STACK is unlocked with a single one-time purchase. Payment is handled by Google Play, so the developer never sees your card details.

To check that you have unlocked the app, including when you reinstall or restore a purchase, STACK uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service that acts on the developer's behalf. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this app, over an encrypted connection. Its policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

STACK does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. No data is sold, and your workout, body-weight and name data is never shared with anyone.

04

Permissions

STACK may ask for permission to send local notifications, used only to remind you about your own workout schedule on your own device. This does not involve sending any information anywhere. You can decline or revoke it at any time in your device's system settings without affecting the app's core logging features. STACK also uses an internet connection, but only to check your purchase.
